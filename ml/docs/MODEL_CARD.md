# Model card template (FaceMaxx)

Every trained artifact carries a generated `MODEL_CARD.md` (see
`facemax_color.training.render_model_card`); this file documents the format
so cards stay complete and comparable. A card is only trustworthy together
with its artifact's `metrics.json` (which holds the numbers it cites).

Per-model cards live at `data/models/<target>/<model_id>/MODEL_CARD.md`
(demo artifacts under `data/demo/models/...` are always marked
`DATA STATUS: SYNTHETIC DEMO` in their reports and `"smoke": true` in their
metadata).

## Required sections

1. **Intended use** — what FaceMaxx uses the model for; explicitly not a
   medical, identity, or attractiveness tool.
2. **Inputs** — the canonical CV feature columns only, plus the note that
   labels/annotator columns/metadata are structurally excluded
   (`LeakageError`).
3. **Outputs** — the target prediction; for season models also the primary
   season, secondary season (documented margin rule), calibrated confidence.
4. **Training data** — source (`real`/`demo`), rows, people,
   `dataset_version` digest, `feature_version`.
5. **Evaluation** — validation strategy (person-grouped StratifiedGroupKFold,
   fold count), CV selection metric and score, the selection evidence
   (runner-up + margin), the feature-group ablation table, locked-holdout
   numbers (accuracy, macro-F1, n, top-2 accuracy), calibration method +
   ECE + Brier (or the explicit insufficiency note).
6. **Known limitations** — small classes, lighting/makeup/quality
   sensitivity, seasonal boundary ambiguity, olive undertone ambiguity,
   flagged features from `feature_audit.json`; smoke artifacts open with
   **"SMOKE TEST ARTIFACT ... NOT FOR PRODUCTION. NOT A BENCHMARK."**;
   never claims beyond the recorded holdout.
7. **Bias / robustness** — per-axis worst/best subgroup and gap from the
   frozen evaluator, small-sample subgroups flagged.
8. **Not intended for** — race, ethnicity, health, identity inference;
   no decisions about individuals beyond colour-analysis styling.

## Example (abridged)

```markdown
# Model card: season-hierarchical_random_forest-v001

## Intended use
FaceMaxx season prediction from CV colour features. ...

## Inputs
Canonical CV features only: feat_skin_L, feat_skin_a, ...

## Training data
- source: real
- rows: 96 from 48 people
- dataset_version: 9f21c6d3a1b04e77
- feature_version: facemax-color-features-v1

## Evaluation
- validation: person-grouped StratifiedGroupKFold (5 folds)
- CV selection metric macro_f1: best=random_forest (0.612)
- locked holdout: accuracy=0.583 macro_f1=0.401 n=24
- calibration: method=sigmoid ECE=0.0721 Brier=0.1834

## Known limitations
- trained on the annotated FaceMaxx dataset only; small classes are unstable
- seasonal boundaries (e.g. Deep Autumn <-> Deep Winter) carry genuine ambiguity
- NOT a benchmark beyond the recorded holdout numbers

## Not intended for
- race, ethnicity, health, attractiveness or identity inference of any kind
```

## Rules

- A card is regenerated by `evaluate` after the holdout run so its numbers
  always match `metrics.json`.
- No card may cite a metric that is not present in the artifact's
  `metrics.json`.
- Demo artifacts must never be quoted as FaceMaxx performance; their cards
  and reports carry the synthetic banner.
